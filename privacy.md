Privacy Policy — Markdown Classic

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Markdown Classic respects your privacy.

We believe your writing should remain private and under your control.

1. Data Collection

Markdown Classic does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

No user accounts or registration are required.

2. Offline Functionality

Markdown Classic is designed to work entirely offline.

An internet connection is not required to use the app.

3. Document Storage

Documents are stored locally on your Mac.

Markdown Classic does not upload your documents to external servers or cloud services.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Markdown Classic does not use analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking tools.

5. Third-Party Services

Markdown Classic does not integrate third-party services that collect personal information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary.

Any changes will be published on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official support page.
